# Meta-policy paper experiments — 2026-08-28

This directory preserves the MuJoCo C++ evidence used for the bilingual paper.
The candidate remains simulation-only and `meta_policy.enabled` remains `false`.

## Fixed acceptance protocol

- Policy rate: 50 Hz.
- Warm-up: 2 s.
- Per-case hold: 6 s for stand/cross/turn, 8 s for straight/diagonal.
- Mixed traversal: 48 s at `vx=0.4 m/s`.
- Maximum relative tilt: 25 degrees.
- Maximum base-height drop: 0.25 m.
- Maximum stand drift: 0.30 m.
- Control outcome and process/teardown outcome are reported separately.

## Layout

- `baseline/`: preflight plus an instrumented mixed-terrain trace.
- `repeats/`: independent 64-case meta-policy matrices.
- `experts/`: isolated Flat, Slope-Up, and Slope-Down matrices.
- `ablations/`: selector replay and closed-loop ablation results.
- `soak/`: repeated 108 s outbound/stop/inbound mixed-lane traversals.
- `figures/`: generated plots derived from recorded traces.
- `reports/`: aggregate machine-readable summaries.

The expert and repeated matrix scripts are `tools/run_expert_acceptance.sh` and
`tools/run_meta_policy_acceptance.sh`. The policy-switch figure is generated by
`tools/plot_meta_policy_switch_trace.py`; replay-only selector ablations are
generated by `tools/replay_selector_ablations.py`.
The compact closed-loop variants are driven by
`tools/run_all_meta_policy_ablations.sh`; the long bidirectional course is
driven by `tools/run_meta_policy_mixed_soak.sh`.

## Evidence boundary

Contract tests, trace replay, and local MuJoCo runs are not hardware acceptance.
Training-history and aggregation ablations require the original three-expert
datasets/checkpoints and an MjLab/PyTorch environment; unavailable inputs must be
reported as blocked rather than reconstructed or silently substituted.
