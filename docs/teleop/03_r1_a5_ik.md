# 03 — R1-A5 Inverse Kinematics

## 1. Arm structure

Mỗi arm R1-A5 có 5 revolute joints:

```text
shoulder_pitch
shoulder_roll
shoulder_yaw
elbow
wrist_roll
```

Joint limits theo asset được audit:

| Joint | Axis | Lower | Upper |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| shoulder pitch | `0 1 0` | −3.1416 | 2.0944 |
| shoulder roll left | `1 0 0` | −0.2269 | 2.4784 |
| shoulder roll right | `1 0 0` | −2.4785 | 0.2268 |
| shoulder yaw | `0 0 1` | −1.9199 | 1.9199 |
| elbow | `0 1 0` | −0.9756 | 2.1852 |
| wrist roll | `1 0 0` | −1.9199 | 1.9199 |

Left/right shoulder-roll range là mirrored.

## 2. 5-DoF constraint

Target wrist pose thuộc $SE(3)$:

$$
T_{EE,d}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
R_d&p_d\\
0&1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

tương đương 6 Cartesian constraints.

Nhưng:

$$
q\in\mathbb{R}^5.
$$

Do đó arbitrary 6-DoF pose không thể luôn được bám chính xác.

## 3. Upstream Unitree R1_A5 IK

Upstream hiện dùng **CasADi + IPOPT nonlinear optimization**, không phải pseudoinverse CLIK thuần.

Operational frame:

$$
p_{EE/wrist}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
0.20\\0\\0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

tức TCP cách `*_wrist_roll_joint` 0.20 m theo local $+x$.

Position error:

$$
e_p(q)
=
\begin{bmatrix}
p_L(q)-p_{L,d}\\
p_R(q)-p_{R,d}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Rotation error:

$$
e_R(q)
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\log(R_L(q)R_{L,d}^T)\\
\log(R_R(q)R_{R,d}^T)
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Objective:

$$
\boxed{
q_k^*
=
\arg\min_q
\left[
50\|e_p\|^2
+
0.5\|e_R\|^2
+
0.02\|q\|^2
+
0.1\|q-q_{k-1}\|^2
\right]
}
$$

subject to:

$$
q_{\min}\le q\le q_{\max}.
$$

Weights upstream:

```text
translation        50
rotation            0.5
regularization      0.02
smoothness          0.1
```

Translation/orientation ratio = 100:1.

Điều này cho thấy upstream chủ động ưu tiên endpoint position mạnh hơn orientation để xử lý 5-DoF limitation.
